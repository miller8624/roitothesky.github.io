Ace Aviation Services · Flight training · Rome, Georgia

From local flight school to online ground school.

Ace teaches pilots at Richard B. Russell Regional Airport (KRMG). I helped bring that instruction online by building the website and video course and managing the school’s social media.

What I handled

  • Course and website buildout
  • Filming and editing
  • Facebook and Instagram
  • TikTok and YouTube

Building and filming the course

I managed the course build from end to end, built the website, and filmed and produced every training video. The course takes students into the hangar and the airplane to see concepts they’ll use in their own training.

Connecting social content to the school

I manage Ace’s presence across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. Short clips give prospective students a chance to see how Ace teaches before exploring the full course.

Creating a new source of revenue

The online ground school gives Ace a way to serve students beyond the airport. Its course subscriptions bring in monthly recurring revenue alongside the school’s in-person instruction.
