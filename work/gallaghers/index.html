Gallagher’s Restaurant · Hospitality · Waterloo, Illinois

From 1,000 to 10,000 email contacts.

Gallagher’s drew people from across the St. Louis area for candied bacon cheeseburgers and Sunday fried chicken. I helped the family-owned restaurant stay in touch with those guests between visits.

~10,000
Email contacts by the end of the family’s run
No paid ads
Used to grow the email list

What I handled

  • Website management
  • Email marketing
  • Branded guest Wi-Fi
  • Menus and social media
  • Paid event ticketing

Giving guests a way to hear from us

I managed the restaurant’s website, menus, email and social media. We also set up branded guest Wi-Fi with an email signup, so people who already enjoyed visiting Gallagher’s could hear about the next dinner or event.

Making special dinners easier to book

I set up online ticketing for paid events so guests could reserve and pay in advance. The restaurant could take bookings before dinner service and use email to tell people when tickets became available.

Growing the list

The email list grew from about 1,000 to about 10,000 contacts without paid ads for list growth. I worked with Gallagher’s during its run as a destination restaurant in Waterloo, before the building sold and a new owner introduced a different restaurant.
